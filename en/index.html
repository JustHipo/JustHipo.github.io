Personal · iOS · Mar – Sep 2026

Jardín

iOS plant-care app with its own watering engine based on evapotranspiration and pot geometry, calibrated with real waterings. It uses on-device AI only to explain and adjust within bounds, and has a widget to log watering without opening the app.

SwiftSwiftUISwiftDataCloudKitFoundationModelsWidgetKit
More
  • Watering engine: reference evapotranspiration (Hargreaves, cross-checked with Open-Meteo), crop coefficient, substrate reserve from pot material and volume, and calibration from real waterings.
  • On-device AI (FoundationModels) explains the calculation and nudges it within a bounded range; without a model on the device, the app still works.
  • SwiftData with CloudKit, shared with the widget through an App Group, and a widget with a water button (App Intents).
  • This is the second version: before rewriting the first one (myGarden) I ran a technical and UX audit against Nielsen's heuristics and Apple's iOS 26 guidelines.

Personal · Research · Sep 2026

fruit-fly

A simulation of the complete fruit fly connectome (161k neurons and 14M synapses) whose activity drives a physical body that paints and a REAPER session that plays music. Every result is checked against networks with shuffled or random wiring.

PythonPyTorchMuJoCoLua
More
  • A spiking (LIF) neuron simulator over the MaleCNS v1.0 connectome, running at 0.107x real time in PyTorch on Apple MPS.
  • art-fly: brain activity drives a pen and then a six-legged 3D body in MuJoCo, with 98% real contact while painting.
  • music-fly: a 4,491-neuron motor subgraph drives a live REAPER session at 33 to 40 Hz.
  • Controls: with shuffled weights activity explodes to 17 to 22 Hz and with a random network it flattens to 3.2 Hz; the real wiring behaves differently, and it shows in the artwork.
